Carrybee is a courier company in Bangladesh. A seller books a parcel, a rider picks it up, it travels hub to hub through the {{term "first-mile-middle-mile-last-mile"}}, and a rider delivers it to the buyer, usually collecting {{term "cod"}}. Carrybee keeps its fees and pays the rest back to the seller.
